- Track: Replit.

## Four equal judging criteria

| Criterion | Strongest proof | Remaining proof needed |
|---|---|---|
| Technological Implementation | Concerning case returns exact Agent Search clauses and request-bound ADK explanation; control returns zero flags | Genuine Replit Agent commit and deployment evidence |
| Design | Light default, Plain/Technical, preserved dismissal reasoning, fixed crisis resources | Signed-out mobile/desktop QA on the Replit URL |
| Potential Impact | Published guidance becomes accessible during drafting without removing writer agency | Qualified independent review of the blinded ten-fragment pack |
| Quality of Idea | No citation means no flag; no score or censor path | Preserve the contested-evidence and human-escalation boundary in Replit changes |

## Ninety-second test

1. Load the guidance case and review it.
2. Open the exact WHO/National Action Alliance sources and inspect the deterministic trigger.
3. Dismiss the note and confirm its evidence remains visible.
4. Load the responsible depiction: zero triggers and zero grounded flags.
5. Open Live Stack: Google Vertex, Agent Search, and ADK should be available; Replit must be true only on the final Replit surface.

Duty of Care is not clinical review, censorship, certification, or proof that a scene is safe.
